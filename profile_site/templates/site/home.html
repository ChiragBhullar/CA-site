Who we are

A multi-disciplinary practice built for scrutiny

K A R S & Co was incorporated in 2019 to provide professional services in Delhi NCR. The practice has since expanded across North India, working directly and through associates in nine cities.

We are equipped to work on the complex problems Indian organisations face across the public and private sectors, combining business, functional and technical knowledge so clients can align their objectives with what the market actually demands.

Our partners each carry five or more years of large-firm experience serving multinational corporations, and the wider team of over twenty professionals covers assurance, taxation, corporate financing, internal audit, IFSC and process drafting.
